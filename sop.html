1到貨前一天Day before delivery

Logistics 會在到貨前一天早上 10:00 寄出「預定隔天到貨的出貨單號與數量」。請依信中的預定數量安排人力。Logistics emails the delivery order numbers and quantities for the next day at 10:00 the day before. Plan staff based on the quantities in the email.
  1. 收信：確認明天要到的出貨單號與件數。Check the email: delivery order numbers and quantities arriving tomorrow.
  2. 依預定數量安排收貨人力、清出後倉空間。Plan staff and clear stockroom space based on the quantities.
Daily Dispatch Viewer 要到到貨當天早上 10:00 才會有該批資料，前一天在 Viewer 上還看不到，請以信件為準。The Daily Dispatch Viewer only shows the shipment from 10:00 on the delivery day; it is not available the day before, so use the email.

2司機到店・簽收Driver arrival & sign-off

先點箱數、看外箱，確認無誤才簽收。簽收單上有箱數，簽了就代表門市確認收到。Count and inspect before you sign. The delivery note shows the carton count; your signature confirms receipt.
  1. 點箱數：實際箱數與司機簽收單上的箱數逐一核對。Count cartons and match them with the carton count on the delivery note.
  2. 檢查外箱：破損、壓扁、受潮、封箱膠帶被拆開或重封。Inspect cartons: torn, crushed, wet, or tape opened / resealed.
  3. 箱數無誤、無破損短少 → 簽收。Count correct, no damage or shortage → sign.
  4. 有任何問題 → 一定要備註在簽收單上再簽（例：應收 5 箱實收 4 箱、第 2 箱外箱破損），並拍下外箱與簽收單備註。Any issue → write it on the delivery note before signing (e.g. 5 cartons expected / 4 received, carton 2 damaged), and photograph the cartons and the note.
簽收單上沒有備註的問題，事後很難追回。司機在場時就要寫清楚。Issues not noted on the delivery note are hard to claim later. Write them down while the driver is there.

3開箱點貨（用 Viewer 掃描比對）Unpack & count with the Viewer

每張出貨單都要逐件掃描比對，不要只點總件數 —— 錯款、錯色、錯尺寸只有掃條碼才看得出來。Scan every item for every delivery order. Wrong style, colour or size only shows up when you scan.
開啟 Viewer Open Viewer ↗
密碼請洽 Logistics。Viewer 當天 10:00 後才有資料、保留最近 7 天；10:00 前到貨請先完成簽收，10:00 後再掃描點貨。Ask Logistics for the password. Data appears after 10:00 on the delivery day and is kept for 7 days. If goods arrive before 10:00, sign for them first and scan after 10:00.
  1. 開啟 Viewer、輸入密碼，在 Dispatch Viewer 分頁選門市、出貨日期，點開這次到貨的出貨單。Open the Viewer and enter the password. In the Dispatch Viewer tab, choose store and ship date, and open the delivery order.
  2. 切到 轉貨單條碼比對 分頁 → ① 轉貨單 按 帶入目前出貨單 → 解析並預覽 → → 確認，開始掃描 →。Switch to the barcode-check tab → step ① load the current delivery order → parse & preview → start scanning.
  3. ② 掃描比對：逐件掃描條碼（掃錯可按 撤銷上一筆）。Step ②: scan every item. Use “undo” if you scan a wrong one.
  4. 全部掃完按 查看差異報告 →，看 ③ 差異報告 是否有短少／溢收。When finished, open the step ③ report to see shortages and overages.
  5. 每張出貨單點完都要按 完成並上傳 Upload，Logistics 才看得到收貨進度（重傳會以最新一次為準）。Press 完成並上傳 Upload for every delivery order so Logistics can see your progress (re-uploading replaces the previous one).
  6. 有差異 → 按 匯出 CSV 報告 存檔，回報時一起附上；同時拍下差異商品。If there are differences, export the CSV report and attach it to your discrepancy report, with photos of the items.
同一次到貨有多張出貨單時，一張一張比對。If several delivery orders arrive together, check them one at a time.

4差異回報（當天）Report discrepancies (same day)

發現差異當天就用「差異回報」頁送出，填寫差異內容並附照片；送出後會直接寄到 Logistics。Submit the discrepancy report on the same day. Fill in the details and attach photos; it is emailed to Logistics directly.
差異類型Type說明Description
短少Shortage實收少於出貨單Fewer items than the delivery order
溢收Overage實收多於出貨單More items than the delivery order
錯貨Wrong item款號、顏色或尺寸與出貨單不同Style, colour or size differs from the order
瑕疵／破損Defect / damage商品本身有瑕疵或運送破損Product defect or damaged in transit
箱數不符／外箱破損Carton count / carton damage簽收時發現，已備註在簽收單Found at sign-off and noted on the delivery note

回報要附的資料What to include

  1. 出貨單號、到貨日期、門市Delivery order number, arrival date, store
  2. 每一筆差異：類型、款號或條碼、顏色、尺寸、應收數量、實收數量Each discrepancy: type, style or barcode, colour, size, expected and received quantity
  3. 照片（必附）：差異商品、吊牌／條碼、外箱；箱數或外箱問題請附簽收單備註照片Photos (required): the items, tags / barcodes, cartons; for carton issues include the delivery-note photo
  4. Viewer 匯出的差異報告 CSV（有的話）The CSV report exported from the Viewer (if any)
開啟差異回報頁 Open discrepancy report ↗

5系統收貨（當天）System receiving (same day)

收到商品當天就要在系統完成收貨。有差異的出貨單也一樣當天收貨，差異另外依第 4 步回報。Complete receiving in the system on the day the goods arrive. Do this even if there is a discrepancy — report it separately under step 4.
系統沒有收貨，庫存不會進到門市帳上，會影響銷售與調撥。Until you receive in the system, the stock is not on your store's books, which affects sales and transfers.

✓收貨完成檢查表Receiving checklist

收貨結束前，逐項確認。Before you finish, check every item.
  1. ☐ 箱數與簽收單一致，異常已備註在簽收單上Carton count matches; issues noted on the delivery note
  2. ☐ 每張出貨單都已用 Viewer 掃描比對並「完成並上傳」Every delivery order scanned in the Viewer and uploaded
  3. ☐ 差異已在當天送出回報（附照片）Discrepancies reported the same day, with photos
  4. ☐ 當天已在系統完成收貨System receiving completed the same day
